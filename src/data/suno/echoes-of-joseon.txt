Korean traditional fusion EDM with afrobeat groove.

Drums: afrobeat groove + Korean traditional rhythm.
Drop: instant hit after build, bass + Korean percussion dominate. Taepyeongso or ajaeng lead within 0.3 sec. Kkwaenggwari for tension only, jing for resonance.
Vocal: female bright and emotional. male deep, thick, heavy and resonant.
Male rap: aggressive, rhythmic, low-register English rap.
Structure: build → drop → chorus. 100-110 BPM.
Atmosphere: traditional + modern + cinematic Joseon night vibe.
Restrictions: no modern synth lead, no weak bass, no thin vocal, traditional instruments must remain clearly recognizable.
