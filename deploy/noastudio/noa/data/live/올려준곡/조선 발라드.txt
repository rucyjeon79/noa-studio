[Intro] [Female Vocal]
(Barely spoken Korean female breath over a single distant daegeum tone and faint gayageum harmonics, as if moonlight has just entered a quiet gyubang.)

방에 남습니다

(Slightly closer vocal, still restrained. Soft janggu heartbeat enters once then fades. Gayageum becomes a little warmer, leaving the last syllable hanging.)

저무는 날에 나는 웃습니다

이 밤의 고요로 나를 채웁니다

(Intimate mid-soft vocal, walking slowly. Gayageum carries the melody in small falling figures. No percussion. Each line given time to settle.)

이 숨만큼은 머물기를

(Voice grows inward rather than louder. Soft ambient room tone. A single haegeum phrase answers the last line like an unspoken name.)

[Chorus 2] [Female Vocal + Haegeum + Daegeum]
(Fuller chorus, still elegant. Haegeum and daegeum braid around the vocal. Subtle low pad and soft bass bed only. The smile in the lyric is audible, not bright.)

저무는 날에 나는 웃습니다

이 밤의 고요로 나를 채웁니다

(Near-whisper Korean female vocal, fragile ornaments, almost no accompaniment except a low geomungo drone. Space feels closer than the verses, as if she is speaking only to the window.)

비로소 그대를 품습니다

창호 앞에서 숨을 고릅니다

[Final Chorus] [Female Vocal + Haegeum + Gayageum + Daegeum]
(Deepest emotion of the song. Vocal opens with han-tinged cry that still stays noble. Traditional ornaments bloom on the long notes. Instruments swell, then leave air around the last hook.)
